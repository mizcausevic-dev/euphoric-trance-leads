Euphoric Trance Leads

Step through one monophonic lead at a time. The notes are read from the MIDI file for the study you pick. Harmony and counterphrase stay off until you ask for them. They are reference parts.

The principal lead is monophonic, MIDI 60 to 84. The harmonic reference uses MIDI 48 to 76, at most four notes at once, and is not part of the core count. The counterphrase is monophonic, MIDI 55 to 81, and is also reference only.

The GitHub repository is https://github.com/mizcausevic-dev/euphoric-trance-leads and it is public. The catalog page is https://mizcausevic-dev.github.io/euphoric-trance-leads/ .

Studies

s01 Lifted Fifth

138 BPM · 16 bars · A natural minor · tonic A

The lead waits an eighth, states E then C then A, answers from a lower E, climbs an Am7 arpeggio, and returns to the fifth on the downbeat. Variation B starts on the downbeat with C and reaches C6 before a late return.

Am for three bars, a rest, F, C, a rest, Am, a rest, F, Em, a rest.

Pair lead A with harmony A and counterphrase A. Variation B is an alternate lead, not a second layer. Harmony and counterphrase are reference files and are not in the core count.

s02 Open Fourth

136 BPM · 16 bars · D dorian · tonic D

A rising fourth opens the phrase, then a rest, then a lower answer. The return flips the fourth so G falls to D. Variation B starts on A and moves the fourth onto the downbeat.

Dm, G, F, then Dm again, each for four bars except a final rest.

Pair each lead variation with the harmony and counterphrase of the same letter. The counterphrase is reference only.

s03 Held Third

140 BPM · 8 bars · F major · tonic F

Long tones carry the major third as the color, with air between the hold and the cadence. Variation B begins on that third and cuts the holds shorter.

F, Bb, C, F, two bars each.

Eight bars, not sixteen. The third is a written pitch, not a synth interval. Pair matching variation letters.

s04 Broken Arc

134 BPM · 16 bars · E natural minor · tonic E

The line climbs by thirds, falls, leaves a bar empty, and returns with the opening third compressed and higher. Variation B starts on B5 and takes its rest earlier.

Em, C, a bar of rest, Am, Em, a bar of rest.

The empty bar is intentional. It is not a missing file. Thirds are not a scale run.

s05 Late Call

132 BPM · 16 bars · B natural minor · tonic B

The call sits on beat 3 until the return, which moves the B-D-F# shape onto the downbeat and up to B5. Variation B opens on the downbeat with a sixth and saves the late call for the return.

Bm, G, A, a rest, Bm, a rest.

Beat 3 is the identity of variation A. Do not quantize it back to the downbeat. Pair matching letters.

s06 Neighbor Turn

137 BPM · 8 bars · G natural minor · tonic G

A neighbor turn around G, a rest, then one flagged F#5 into G5. Variation B turns downward to F and stays inside the collection, with a different rhythm.

Gm, Eb, a rest, Gm.

The F# is one flagged departure in variation A only. Do not treat it as part of the collection. Eight bars.

s07 Wide Leap

135 BPM · 16 bars · C major without B · tonic C

A leap from C4 to A5, a rest, and a two-note answer. The return leaps downward. Variation B leaps from C5 down to E4 and brings C6 in after a rest.

C, F, Am, a rest, C, a rest. No B is written.

B is outside the collection and is not used. The leap is the motif. Do not fill it with passing tones.

s08 Soft Return

139 BPM · 8 bars · F# natural minor · tonic F#

A soft call on beat 2, a peak on B5, a bar of rest, and a return an octave lower. Variation B starts low on the downbeat and saves the high B for late in the phrase.

F#m, D, a rest, F#m.

Eight bars. The octave return is written out. It is not a transpose of the whole file. Pair matching letters.
